Niazaa Technology (Private) Limited is a SECP-registered company operating under the Niazaa Group of Business. Founded in August 2023 by Mudassar Niaz and officially registered in 2024, the company delivers innovative, reliable, and cost-effective technology solutions across Pakistan.
